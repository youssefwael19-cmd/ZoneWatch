Auto-trading · Exness via MetaTrader 5

Places the app's own signals as orders. It starts on a demo: paper mode needs nothing but a balance figure, bridge mode talks to MetaTrader 5 through the small service in bridge/, and direct mode calls a broker's own API (OANDA) from the cloud so no machine has to stay on. It will only ever trade a market the brain gate has approved, never more than one position at a time per market, and it stops for the day once the loss limit is hit.

Spread is modelled per market (gold 0.30, Nasdaq 1.5 points, majors ~1.2 pips) and charged on entry and exit, so paper results are net of the broker rather than flattered by free trading.
No terminal and no VPS: the app places the order over the broker's own API and the broker holds the stop and target. cTrader needs a registered application (client ID + secret) and an account that has approved it — the access token lasts about a month, so supply a refresh token and the app renews it by itself. Practice and live are separate hosts. A size below the broker's minimum volume is refused rather than rounded up, because rounding up would multiply the risk.
TradingView cannot place orders — it can only POST an alert to a URL you own. Point the alert's webhook at /api/tv/webhook?token=…, and make the message either JSON {"symbol":"XAUUSD","action":"buy","entry":4010,"stop":3990,"target":4050} or one line: BUY XAUUSD entry=4010 stop=3990 target=4050.

Automation

When this is on, nothing is sent at the weekend, during the daily rollover gap, or when the price feed has stopped ticking. The app keeps tracking zones the whole time, so it picks up where it left off.
The strictest filter in the app. With this on, a zone touch or signal is only sent when the brain has shown some rule makes money in this market, in the kind of market it is right now — it will often mean silence, which is the intent. Everything withheld appears under Held back in the Learning tab with its reason. Turn it off to get every setup again.
Signals are rule-based confluence, not backtested, and not financial advice.

Markets watched

Quiet hours (UTC)

Quiet hours suppress Discord alerts only — the watcher keeps tracking zones so nothing is missed.

Correlated markets

Markets on the same line move together, so only one alert is sent for each group — otherwise your phone buzzes twice for the same trade. Write one group per line, separated by commas. A symbol needs a partner to form a group; single symbols are ignored.

Uptime monitoring

The app cannot tell you it is dead — a stopped app sends nothing. This fixes that the other way round: the app pings an external service, and that service alerts you when the pings stop.

Uptime monitor endpoint for other services: /healthz returns 200 while the watcher is alive and recently checked the market, and 503 if it is stuck.

Strategy lab

The app tests its own signals against a year of history every day — tuning on older data, scoring only on data it has never seen. Signal alerts are only sent for markets where that test finds an edge. It is allowed to conclude “no edge”.
These alerts are sent with an ⚠️ UNPROVEN label and the lab's verdict, so they can never be mistaken for a validated edge.
